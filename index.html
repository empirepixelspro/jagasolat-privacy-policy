Privacy Policy — Jaga Solat

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Developer: Empire Pixels Pro

Jaga Solat (“the app”), developed by Empire Pixels Pro, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

Information the app accesses

  • Location (approximate & precise): Used only on your device to detect your prayer-time zone (JAKIM e-Solat Malaysia or global calculation) and to show nearby mosques/surau and the Qibla direction. Location data is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Calendar: Optional. With your permission, the app reads your device calendar to display public holidays and to sync prayer/widget events. Calendar data stays on your device.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Optional. With your permission, the app schedules prayer reminders, azan alarms and follow-up notifications.
  • Internet: Used to fetch official JAKIM e-Solat prayer times, weather data, and to load advertisements.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, location, calendar contents or device identifiers to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone or storage beyond what is stated above.

Advertising

The app may display Google AdMob ads. Google may collect advertising identifiers to serve personalised ads, subject to Google’s own policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

In-app purchases

Payments for Infaq / Pro Lifetime products (RM5, RM10, RM30) are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.

Data deletion

All app data (prayer records, qada records, zikir counters, settings) is stored locally on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app, or use in-app “Reset” options. To request removal of any licence-key record, email us with your device identifier.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
