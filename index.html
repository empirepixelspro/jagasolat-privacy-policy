Jaga Solat Android

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jaga Solat (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. Jaga Solat has no backend server — all of your core data stays safely on your device. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Target Audience & Age Appropriateness

Jaga Solat is intended for a general audience of all ages. The application provides tools for daily prayer tracking, Qibla direction, and Islamic resources suitable for the entire family. We do not collect or harvest personal identifying information from any users, regardless of age.

2. Geographical Distribution Exclusion

Please note that this application is distributed globally through the Google Play Store, with the explicit exception of Israel (IL). The service is not licensed or supported for use within this region.

3. Information the App Accesses

The app accesses the following only on your device, and only with your permission where required:

  • Location (approximate & precise): Used strictly on-device to determine your prayer-time zone (JAKIM e-Solat Malaysia or global calculation), Qibla direction, and nearby mosques/surau. Location data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Calendar (optional): With your permission, reads your device calendar to show public holidays and sync prayer or widget events locally on your device.
  • Notifications & Alarms (optional): Schedules prayer reminders, azan alarms, and follow-up notifications.
  • Internet: Used to fetch official JAKIM e-Solat prayer times, weather data, and to load advertisements.
  • What we do NOT collect: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, location history, calendar contents, or unique device identifiers to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To display accurate prayer times and calculate your Qibla direction.
  • To send prayer reminders and azan alarms you configure.
  • To power your home-screen widgets and holiday calendar.
  • To process voluntary digital contributions securely via Google Play Billing (RM5 / RM10 / RM30 Infaq) — payment details are never seen or stored by us.

5. Advertising

Jaga Solat may display Google AdMob advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. No other data is shared with advertisers.

6. Data Security & Deletion

All app data (prayer records, qada records, zikir counters, and settings) is stored locally on your device, and any network traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Deletion: Users retain full control over their data. You can erase all local data instantly by using the in-app "Reset" options or by uninstalling the app. To request removal of any technical support or license-key records, contact us by email with your identifier.
