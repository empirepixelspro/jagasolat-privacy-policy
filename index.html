Jaga Solat Android

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jaga Solat (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. Jaga Solat has no backend server — all of your data stays on your device. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information the App Accesses

The app accesses the following only on your device, and only with your permission where required:

  • Location (approximate & precise): Used on-device to determine your prayer-time zone (JAKIM e-Solat Malaysia or global calculation), Qibla direction and nearby mosques/surau. Location is never transmitted to us.
  • Calendar (optional): With your permission, reads your device calendar to show public holidays and sync prayer/widget events. Calendar data stays on your device.
  • Notifications & Alarms (optional): With your permission, schedules prayer reminders, azan alarms and follow-up notifications.
  • Internet: Used to fetch official JAKIM e-Solat prayer times, weather data and to load advertisements.
  • What we do NOT collect: We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, location, calendar contents or device identifiers to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display accurate prayer times and calculate your Qibla direction.
  • To send prayer reminders and azan alarms you configure.
  • To power your home-screen widgets and holiday calendar.
  • To process In-app purchases securely via Google Play Billing (RM5 / RM10 / RM30 Infaq) — payment details are never seen or stored by us.

3. Advertising

Jaga Solat may display Google AdMob advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. No other data is shared with advertisers.

4. Data Security & Deletion

All app data (prayer records, qada records, zikir counters, settings) is stored locally on your device, and any network traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

To delete your data: uninstall the app, or use the in-app “Reset” options. To request removal of a licence-key record, contact us by email with your device identifier.
